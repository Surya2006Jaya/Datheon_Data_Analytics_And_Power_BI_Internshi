# Weekly Projects

## Week 1 — Exploratory Data Analysis on Superstore Retail Sales

### Objective

Analyze retail sales transactions to understand customer purchasing behavior, monthly sales seasonality, category-wise revenue, and discount sensitivity.

### Key Activities

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Handle missing values and validate sales records.
- Parse order dates and extract Year-Month features.
- Analyze sales and profit by product category.
- Evaluate regional and customer segment performance.
- Visualize monthly sales trends.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Formulate at least five executive business findings.

### Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

### Deliverables

- Cleaned data analysis notebook.
- Sales and profit visualizations.
- Monthly sales trend analysis.
- Discount sensitivity analysis.
- Executive business findings.

**[View Week 1 Project](./Week-1-Superstore-EDA/)**

## Week 2 — Interactive Power BI Executive Sales & Profit Dashboard

### Objective

Transform raw retail sales data into an interactive executive dashboard that supports business performance monitoring and decision-making.

### Key Activities

- Connect Power BI to the Superstore dataset.
- Transform data using Power Query.
- Design a Star Schema data model.
- Create Fact Orders, Dim Customer, Dim Product, and Dim Date tables.
- Develop DAX measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Year-over-Year Sales Growth.
- Build KPI scorecards.
- Create regional performance treemaps.
- Develop product category and subcategory profitability matrices.
- Implement dynamic date and region slicers.
- Create monthly sales visualizations.
- Export a high-resolution executive dashboard report.

### Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling

### Deliverables

- Interactive Power BI dashboard (`.pbix`).
- Exported executive dashboard PDF.
- Dashboard screenshots.
- Data model and DAX documentation.

**[View Week 2 Project](./Week-2-PowerBI-Sales-Dashboard/)**

## Week 3 — Customer Cohort & Churn Analytics

### Objective

Analyze customer retention behavior using the IBM Telco Churn dataset to identify customer churn patterns, retention trends, and high-risk customer segments.

### Key Activities

- Load and explore the Telco Churn dataset.
- Calculate the baseline customer churn rate.
- Segment customers by tenure and contract type.
- Analyze customer cohorts based on subscription vintage.
- Investigate churn patterns across payment methods.
- Evaluate the relationship between monthly charges and churn.
- Identify high-risk customer segments.
- Analyze churn among Fiber Optic users and customers without technical support.
- Develop cohort retention matrices.
- Create churn heatmaps or Power BI visualizations.
- Formulate strategic customer retention recommendations.

### Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, Power BI, Excel, Data Visualization

### Deliverables

- Customer cohort retention matrix.
- Churn analysis report.
- Customer segment visualizations.
- High-risk customer analysis.
- Strategic retention recommendations.

**[View Week 3 Project](./Week-3-Customer-Cohort-Churn/)**

## Week 4 — Corporate Financial & Margin Performance Dashboard

### Objective

Develop a financial analytics dashboard to monitor quarterly revenue, cost of goods sold, discounts, and operating margins across corporate divisions.

### Key Activities

- Connect Power BI to the Microsoft Financial Sample dataset.
- Develop an income statement hierarchy.
- Analyze Gross Sales, Discounts, Net Sales, COGS, and Profit.
- Perform actual-versus-budget variance analysis.
- Evaluate financial performance across geographic segments.
- Analyze product lines and manufacturing cost centers.
- Develop DAX measures for Gross Profit Margin, COGS Ratio, and Segment Margin.
- Create a waterfall chart to analyze revenue reduction through discounts and manufacturing expenses.
- Implement interactive scenario forecasting sliders.
- Develop a financial KPI dashboard.

### Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop, DAX, Financial Modeling, Data Visualization

### Deliverables

- Financial KPI dashboard (`.pbix`).
- Financial performance analysis.
- Variance analysis.
- Interactive scenario modeling.
- Financial dashboard documentation.

**[View Week 4 Project](./Week-4-Financial-Margin-Dashboard/)**
