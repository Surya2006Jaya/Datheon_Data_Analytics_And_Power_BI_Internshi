# Week 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Superstore Retail Sales

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Superstore Retail Sales dataset as part of my Datheon Data Analytics & Power BI Internship.

The objective is to analyze customer purchasing behavior, monthly sales seasonality, category-wise revenue, regional performance, and discount sensitivity to identify meaningful business insights.

## Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Inspect and handle missing values.
- Convert order dates and extract Year-Month features.
- Analyze sales and profit by product category.
- Evaluate regional and customer segment performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends and seasonality.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Formulate at least five executive business findings.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The Superstore Retail Sales dataset contains retail transaction information, including:

- Order Date
- Customer Segment
- Product Category
- Region
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit

The dataset was inspected and cleaned before performing the analysis.
